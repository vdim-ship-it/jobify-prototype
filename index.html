HOW IT WORKS

  1. 1

    Tell us your goal

    Choose the kind of work you want and where you are today.

  2. 2

    Complete real projects

    Work on tasks based on what employers and clients actually need.

  3. 3

    Get mentor feedback

    Industry professionals review your work and tell you how to improve it.

  4. 4

    Show your work, get matched

    Build a portfolio and resume, then see jobs and freelance work that fit.

WHO IT IS FOR

Built for young people starting out

Recent graduates

You have the degree but no work experience yet. Jobify gives you real projects to point to in your first applications.

Self-taught, no degree

You are learning on your own and want to earn. Jobify helps you prove your skills and find freelance or entry-level work.

OUR MISSION

To equip young Nigerians with high-quality, in-demand skills within the next three to ten years, so they can increase their earning power and build sustainable income.
